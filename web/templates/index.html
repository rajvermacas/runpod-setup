Field guide — what each preset does

Pose

  • full-body — head to feet, standing, camera steps back
  • sitting — seated naturally, three-quarter view
  • walking — mid-stride toward camera, clothes and hair in motion
  • laughing — caught mid-laugh, unposed expression
  • over-shoulder — looking back, body turned away
  • sitting-floor — on the ground, elbows on knees, slouched
  • doing-thing — mid-activity, hands busy, candid

Style (light)

  • snapshot-flash — phone-flash at night, slightly harsh, most realistic signal
  • overcast-day — flat daylight, even tones, calm
  • golden-hour — warm low sun, natural glow
  • indoor-lamp — warm room light, cozy evening
  • film-grain — analog grain over everything, kills smoothness globally

Scene (background)

  • street / market — city life behind, blurred people and shops
  • cafe — coffee cup, casual interior
  • park — trees and path, daylight
  • home — lived-in room, sofa and window light
  • beach-day — midday beach, sea and distant people

Lens

  • 24mm — dramatic wide, face fills frame
  • 35mm — environmental, context stays
  • 50mm — natural, eye-level, neutral
  • 85mm — tight portrait, blurred background
  • 135mm — flat telephoto, creamy distance

Light

  • soft-window — gentle one-side daylight, calm
  • neon-night — cyan-magenta glow, dark streets
  • harsh-noon — hard overhead sun, crisp shadows
  • firelight — warm flicker, dark background
  • blue-hour — deep twilight, quiet mood
  • studio-softbox — clean controlled key light
